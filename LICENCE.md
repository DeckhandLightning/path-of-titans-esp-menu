## ✨ Features

### ESP
- Resource Markers — highlight nearby food and water points
- Nest Locator — map notes for nesting sites
- Growth Tracker — review growth progress in single-player
- Stamina Notes — study stamina usage per dino

### Wallhack
- Map Overlay — world map markers and zone notes
- Waypoint Notes — save and review travel routes
- Danger Zone Notes — mark predator territories for study
- Coordinate Display — on-screen position readout

### Aimbot
- Dino Catalog — browse species stats and traits
- Skin Notes — organize cosmetic preferences
- Config Manager — save overlay presets
- Hotkey Panel — toggle modules quickly
